## Slide 2

**Our Business and Target Customers**

- Bencris is a karinderya (small eatery) in Dasmariñas Bayan, Cavite
- We sell 23 home-cooked ulam recipes plus rice
- Our customers are nearby residents, students, and workers who want an affordable lunch
- The menu shown online is the food actually in the kitchen right now

---

## Slide 3

**Competitive Advantage and Profit Strategy**

- Every dish is priced from its real recipe, not a guess
- 25 dishes, about 17% profit per batch
- Ingredient prices are checked regularly against real suppliers
- Example: Chicken Curry dropped 78 pesos a batch once we priced the curry powder correctly

---

## Slide 4

**Top Security Risks and How We Protect Against Them**

- Wrong access rules: only the right person can see each order
- Fake payment proof: an order cannot be paid twice, and receipt links expire
- Stolen staff password: staff role is set by the shop, never by the app
- We found and fixed one real access bug during testing
- Next step: stronger password rules before real accounts go live

---

## Slide 5

**Payment Process and Verification**

- Customers can pay with Cash or GCash
- Customer browses the menu and gets a ticket code
- For GCash, the customer pays and uploads a receipt
- The cashier checks the payment before marking it Paid
- No outside company handles the money
